FAQ

Questions we get a lot

Do I need to own my own servers?

No. You can bring existing hardware, or we can source and rack refurbished or new equipment sized to your workload and budget.

How is this different from just using the cloud?

For steady, predictable workloads — file servers, ERP, databases, video archives — owning the hardware and colocating it is typically 40–60% cheaper over three years. Many clients run a hybrid: colo for the baseline, cloud for bursts.

What happens if something fails at 2am?

Our monitoring alerts our on-call engineer before you notice. Remote hands are on site 24/7 for reboots, recabling and drive swaps, and you get an incident summary the next morning.

Can you help us pass a security audit?

Yes. We provide facility attestations, access logs, network diagrams and change records that map to common SOC 2, HIPAA and PCI control requirements.

What are the contract terms?

Month-to-month, 12-month and 36-month terms are available. Longer terms waive setup fees and lock your rate.
