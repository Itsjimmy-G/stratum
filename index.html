05 — FAQ

Questions we get on every first call.

Something not covered here? Mail hello@stratum.ai and an engineer replies — not a bot.

Do I need to own my own servers?

No. Bring existing hardware, or we source and rack new or refurbished equipment sized to your workload and budget.

How is this different from just using the cloud?

For steady, predictable workloads — file servers, ERP, databases, video archives — owning the hardware and colocating it typically costs 40–60% less over three years. Most clients run a hybrid: colo for the baseline, cloud for bursts.

What happens if something fails at 2am?

Monitoring pages our on-call engineer before you notice. Remote hands are on site around the clock for reboots, recabling and drive swaps, and you get an incident summary the next morning.

Can you help us pass a security audit?

Yes. We supply facility attestations, access logs, network diagrams and change records that map to common SOC 2, HIPAA and PCI control requirements.

What are the contract terms?

Month-to-month, 12-month and 36-month terms. Longer terms waive setup fees and lock your rate for the duration.

Can we visit our hardware?

Any time, with 24 hours' notice for badge provisioning. Escorted access is available same-day for emergencies.
